## TRAVEL ENCOUNTER D100 — PLAYER-APPROVED (Day223 OOC; prospective only)
Scope: for ordinary travel not itself an active quest encounter. Convergence-derived creatures are ordinary established regional ecology after ~1,200 years, not an excuse for constant random portals. Routes, patrols, terrain, monster populations and local political situation determine a declared risk tier BEFORE any dice. Traveling guards, scouts, patrols, safe lodgings and route choice make practical differences; guarded roads are safer, not immune. This procedure does not replay the Day216–223 Caereth journey; its previously reported d20 travel rolls were not mapped to a fixed outcome table or independently verified as genuine RNG. No campaign time passes from adopting these OOC rules.
1. FREQUENCY: On each day of ordinary overland journey, roll one real uniform d100 for the daytime encounter check. If no encounter triggers and the party sleeps in an exposed wilderness/roadside camp, roll one additional d100 for the night; do NOT roll a camping check within a genuinely secured town/fortress. An event during the daytime fills that day's maximum; skip the night check. Otherwise at most ONE random travel encounter per travel day including its following night. The reported percentages below are individual check probabilities, NOT guaranteed proportions of encounters, combat or safe travel.
2. RISK TIERS (d100; inclusive on trigger):
- Heavily patrolled road: DAY 01–07 = 7%; EXPOSED NIGHT 01–03 = 3%.
- Ordinary trade road: DAY 01–15 = 15%; EXPOSED NIGHT 01–07 = 7%.
- Frontier/contested country: DAY 01–30 = 30%; EXPOSED NIGHT 01–20 = 20%.
- Remote wilderness road: DAY 01–40 = 40%; EXPOSED NIGHT 01–30 = 30%.
- Known monster territory or dangerous LOCAL Convergence seam: DAY 01–50 = 50%; EXPOSED NIGHT 01–40 = 40%.
No monster-country tier by vague proximity to all Convergence history. Select tier separately as geography/patrol/terrain actually changes; do not quietly shift it because V has rested or combat is 'due'. A monster encounter is not automatically a fight, and some days are genuinely uneventful.
3. TWO CONSECUTIVE DAYS MAXIMUM: If two successive TRAVEL DAYS each had a random travel encounter, the next ordinary travel day is a mandatory quiet day for random encounters (skip both occurrence rolls), then reset consecutive streak. Quest scenes, known pursuing enemies and independently scheduled events are not secretly cancelled by this protection; this is strictly for RANDOM TRAVEL ENCOUNTERS. One random travel event in a day maximum irrespective of day/night.
4. 70% CAP FOR NON-QUEST TRAVEL: On a planned multi-day travel leg with N expected travel days, allow no more than floor(0.70 × N) travel DAYS containing random encounters, count daytime and night encounter together as ONE encounter day. Declare the leg's intended length at departure and its cap; adjust fairly if route/time truly changes, never alter past rolls. Once the cap is reached, remaining travel days in that leg are quiet for random travel encounters. On open-ended journeys without a reliable N, enforce a rolling ten-travel-day window of at most seven encounter days and the two-consecutive rule; do not fabricate a fixed route length. SINGLE-DAY journeys may have one encounter because a 70% per-journey cap would mathematically prohibit all one-day encounters; this is an explicit short-trip exception. Do not break a route into artificial 'legs' to increase encounter frequency. Cap concerns random encounters on ordinary travel only, not encounters in actual active adventures/quests, consequences of V's choices or established independent actors.
5. ENCOUNTER TYPE (roll second genuine d100 only on occurrence):
01–42 (42%) monster, dangerous beast or other Convergence-descended creature;
43–59 (17%) dangerous humanoid group/organized predator;
60–76 (17%) material environmental hazard or dangerous aftermath;
77–92 (16%) people, traders, patrols or local social encounter;
93–100 (8%) unusual discovery or regional world event.
Creature category means a genuine independent creature with habitat, food, reason to be there, sensory awareness, goals and retreat/avoidance options; no guaranteed initiative. Monster choice must use a REGIONAL TABLE/eligible list, fixed before result selection, not an invented retroactive meaning for a d100. An ecological monster can be nonhostile, hunting, protecting offspring, wounded or attacking.
6. HOSTILE ENCOUNTER SEVERITY (third real d100 only IF actually hostile encounter; difficulty estimated with V's ACTUAL participating party and 2014 rules): 01–30 manageable (30%), 31–75 serious (45%), 76–95 deadly (20%), 96–100 overwhelming (5%). These are design/tactical-intent bands, not secretly guaranteed outcomes or mandatory combat. Set concrete opponents, numbers, terrain, relative distances, motives, detection and retreat paths BEFORE any combat or additional roll. An overwhelming threat needs truthful observable danger and an actionable chance to avoid or escape, not a forced unwinnable surprise. No automatic downscaling/fudging after dice.
LEVEL PREFLIGHT: use actual attendance with RULES DM procedures' current V-level design baseline, then resolve using each participant's actual live statistics/resources. The level7 review changes no probability, encounter-day cap, quiet-day rule or predeclared regional outcome. Do not replace a previously fixed creature after seeing a severity roll or the party's tactics.
7. FAIR EXECUTION/LOGGING: Fix tier, regional candidate table, occurrence thresholds, calendar/time/campsite, and any safety/cap override BEFORE using executed genuine RNG (Python secrets or comparable real source; disclose unavailability). Show relevant rolled d100 FACE and table outcome. Do not invent weather or road-event tables after rolling. Weather is handled separately by established geography/season, with a predeclared separate table if rolled; benign descriptive weather is not an encounter. A triggered event involving NPCs/monster must have plausible independent causes, and the encounter is presented as a playable actionable scene rather than forcing V's action. Preserve 2014 perception/surprise/cover, encounter avoidance, resource/Long Rest interruption rules and player control; ordinary sightings and conversations need not interrupt rests.
