## No-block ordinary-play lane (priority)

For **routine, low-stakes turns** such as a companion voluntarily accompanying an NPC, ordinary conversation, moving within an already-known settlement, buying standard food/feed, or ordinary inventory top-ups: use the retained verified scene packet, decide independent NPC consent, apply established prices/quantities and update an **in-conversation unsynced ledger**. **Narrate and return the next actionable scene immediately**; do not fetch full documents or perform GitHub writing, dashboard rebuild, quest audit, magic-shop turnover, art retrieval, or exhaustive status reconciliation merely because position, time, a few coins or provisions changed. Ordinary purchases may execute under the player's explicit resupply direction and previously established practical targets; never auto-buy extraordinary items or exceed authorization.

An ordinary town errand is **not by itself** significant exploration, a major transaction, a quest milestone, or a mandatory synchronization boundary. Substantive NPC discoveries/decisions are preserved in the in-conversation ledger but need not cause immediate writes until an actual milestone, substantial scene break, about8–12 substantive changed exchanges, requested pause/end/Sync campaign, or another listed sync threshold. Avoid repeating the same per-turn delivery/visual preflight once known current actors and setting are verified. Keep NPC independence and genuine material stakes; the fast path does NOT force agreement, waive meaningful checks or deny consequences.

On a genuine sync boundary, preflight and verify the guarded owner batch, then commit CAMPAIGN_STATE last. Resume narration without waiting for CI or repairs; report unsynced owner deltas separately from pending derived verification. Same-chat accepted play remains authoritative over a temporarily older persisted packet; do not re-fetch stale PLAY_NOW or reset it between routine turns. **If the user opens a new chat before a boundary sync, unsynced in-conversation changes may not be recoverable**: do not claim they were saved. Always sync on explicit pause/end or when told a new chat is coming. Do not perform work after a chat closes.

## Fast magic browse — read-first, no compulsory admin

A V inquiry ("anything magical worth buying?") is **discovery/browsing only**, not consent to purchase, fund commissions, place holds or run a market-maintenance audit. With an already prepared/discovered settlement, first use retained current-scene market offers and documented Dealers/Stock/Events; give **2–5 grounded, relevant offers** with actual asking prices, item effects, scarcity/attunement if known, and a short usefulness assessment for V/party. Do not reroll vendors, repeat source research, bulk fetch shop CSVs, open 12 unrelated websites, redo market turnover, recalculate estates, poll CI, or write GitHub just to show known offers. V chooses later.

For an **unprepared** settlement, follow the one-time Rural market roll and eligible R01–R30 Templates using genuine RNG and local plausibility; keep the result stable in the in-conversation unsynced ledger until normal sync. Prefer candidates with source/description already verified in the existing pool. Full 5etools/Griffon breadth informs **candidate preparation and periodic market maintenance**, not every live browse. If a selected item's exact mechanics are missing, do one narrow source check (prefer the recorded original source), disclose what remains unverified, and offer without invented effects; do not undertake multi-site research during the player's turn. No committed sale/hold from an inquiry. At natural sync, batch the actual discovery/market changes with affected owners once and commit CAMPAIGN_STATE last. A market-only change must not trigger comprehensive estate/Blackthorn/tax validation.
