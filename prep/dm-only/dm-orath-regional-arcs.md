## Reward design and delivery contract
Substantial independent adventures below have proposed cash budgets of 250–700gp; regional resolutions generally 1,200–3,000gp. These are finite **whole-party** design budgets, not per-person payouts, level-scaled formulas, existing public bounties, or money already created/transferred. Each entry identifies who would own/pay the reward and what success buys. Before offer, lock a particular authorized patron, available funds, payment milestones and lawful salvage terms. If a proposed payer cannot support the sum, use a credible co-sponsor, change the unoffered objective or retain HOLD; never make poor villagers secretly wealthy, seize victim property, charge V's estates, or promise unfunded pay.

A permanent magic item can replace a stated cash award by an explicit, worthwhile bargain. Before that option is offered, select and verify an exact permitted 2014-family item, owner/custody, actual transfer authority, attunement/limitations and recipient choice. No unspecified 'rare item' certificate, guaranteed shop stock, custom power, healing potion or automatic claim to another person's heirloom. Current drafts use cash so no unverified item is implied available. Major regional treasure may be a lawful finite cache in a new site only after its ownership and contents are fixed before entry. Recovering stolen goods is not automatic salvage.

An early partial success pays its agreed milestone even if V declines the wider plot. Reveal a meaningful reward and concrete stakes at the real offer; do not make the player spend days before learning that nobody can pay. Quiet social scenes can remain unpaid life; the substantial quests requested here should not routinely conclude with gratitude alone. Existing accepted prices, unpaid rescues, refusals and historical rewards stay unchanged.

## Activation, discovery and endings
These are a connected landscape, not a compulsory itinerary. Each regional unit must have:
1. A primary early source with direct knowledge, physical location, ordinary activity and a practical first step; a second source that does not depend on the first surviving; and environmental/physical corroboration where a revelation matters.
2. An understandable local objective and substantial stated reward, plus a real alternative or refusal. A capital is never a mandatory hub for every countryside job.
3. An independent actor record: objective, actual knowledge, finite means, next move, act/wait/stop conditions. Dates are set from causes and confirmed time, not daily real-world runs or V arriving. No activated disaster, attack or messenger in this outline.
4. A local full endpoint and a paid partial endpoint. A wider lead is optional after closure. Declining a sequel does not void payment, rescue or safety.
5. An ordinary arrival activity, reachable accommodation/care and a non-quest interaction. Do not make the continent an uninterrupted chain of crises.

For each proposed regional connection, provide a plain answer once evidence supports it. Witnesses may establish local conduct without knowing the mastermind; a regional institution may offer help without possessing secret answers. No universal symbols as empty teasers. No clue may be inserted into a previously searched place. All countries remain populated by ordinary people with interests beyond these plots.

**Approach regression applied to the design pattern, not all tactical encounters:** direct question gets the source's real problem and terms; an alternate sponsor/route can change risk; refusal ends that invitation without a replacement messenger; failed checks cost disclosed time/opportunity, not all essential knowledge; missing witness can be replaced by already-fixed physical evidence or a genuinely independent observer; early rescue/negotiation can bypass the planned fight; local closure earns its milestone without following the main arc. These are implementation requirements to recheck in the selected packet, not fictional playtest results.
