DIRECTIVE GLASS SPEAR — THE THREE PROTAGONISTS' DIVINE LOSSES (DM ONLY)
Latest explicit player canon override: responsibility established, discovery unplayed
The Veyran Western Program DEFINITIVELY orchestrated and was causally responsible for the killings of the three divine figures whose deaths Severed current protagonists: (A) V/Valenthia Vaishnavi's direct GOD PARENT (not his vampire father), (B) Cirren Seventh's direct GOD PARENT, and (C) NOBANION, Fenar Raukhar's distant divine forebear whose unusually potent descendant bond caused her own Severing. All three are real Godslayings; this is settled DM truth, not an in-world suspicion, conditional proposal, or evidence of actions by the three protagonists. Later live clues will let the characters discover and prove it. This player decision overrides older provisional source language saying their deaths are not assumed to share a perpetrator. It does NOT retroactively change any scene, speech, dice, resource or current quest.
Public/in-character knowledge remains unchanged: the victims of V's and Cirren's direct divine parentage are not identified by name in current played sources; Nobanion is confirmed dead about SIX MONTHS BEFORE DAY171, and Fenar felt his last painful roar while on a mountain hunt, was injured and lost prior power. The exact date/location/means of all three lethal acts are not known by the party. Nobanion was not Fenar's literal parent: he was a DISTANT divine ancestor with a specific demigod-like inherited potency. The affected gods are physically killable, but their deaths naturally shattered essence and independently caused the characters' Severing. No Program spell caused the Severing itself; no fatal-essence transfer or automatic magical artifact occurred.
Not responsible by this override: V's vampire father's disappearance (still open); the birth or original noble status of House Vaishnavi; the Convergence-era calamities; Bane's First Mortal Godslaying roughly 950 years ago; Lathander's death ~300 years ago; every other recent god death; the Graven Assembly, Miregate Undertow Compact, Cirren's mentor/Quenwood case, Eastwatch Covenant/Seventh Seat, Blackthorn ogre zombie, Fenar's family settlement, or the Ashen Regency's crimes. Recent Godslayings by DIFFERENT actors remain world canon; the Program caused these THREE linked PC tragedies only. The Iron Dominion and Ashen Regency are not co-sponsors.
Covert command: Rhelka and Corven, not an omnipotent godslayer
Program designation: DIRECTIVE GLASS SPEAR, a HIGHLY RESTRICTED, TARGET-SPECIFIC SERIES of three god-interdiction orders using compartmentalized military intelligence, mortal strike cadres, limited magical sabotage and local proxy forces. The Western Program's sinister research and national-interest ideology motivated the project; it did not possess a reusable automatic god-killing ray, divine nullification aura or portable hand-of-Bane GPS. No god was killed by a lone level-six officer, single surgeon or Helmed Horror. Each deity was assaulted under distinct, exceptional conditions and with substantial risk, casualties and local actors. Those particular historical mixed strike cadres are not present-day extra fighters, respawning reinforcements or a new secret standing army.
Field Commissioner RHELKA SOUD personally approved and directed the mortal operational/deniable logistics for all three GLASS SPEAR missions during the Program's recent operational era. She knows they succeeded in killing the three intended divine targets, and she has intentionally concealed civilian deaths, destroyed evidence and used proxies. She does NOT automatically know which mortal descendants were Severed, that V survives after a pre-Convergence burial, that Cirren lives in the party, or that Fenar is a potent descendant. Her timeline of knowledge must track documents and physical messengers. Her culpability is fixed and cannot be washed away by blaming a rogue scientist.
Director-Physician CORVEN NASK supervised the observation protocol, god-residue analysis and coercive follow-on trials connected to the three missions, and helped design the finite arcane suppression/containment attempts that made human intervention conceivable. He received separate field results, not instant omniscient witnessing. He KNOWS the Program killed gods and is complicit in deadly tests on mortal captives. One or more field attacks succeeded despite his experimental theories being partially wrong; the Program is fallible. His research is related to Evermarch/Clear Will but is NOT the Covenant's technology and does not automatically reproduce a deity's power.
Director SERA VEYT approved a classified military 'divine risk elimination' budget line and receives high-level results; what she knew of each TARGET, exact atrocity and unlawful method beyond that classified mandate is evidence-dependent and must be specified by actual documents before any confrontation. First Marshal ILYRA VASK and Marshal DEREN KORR retain their preexisting roles and policies; do not assert direct authorization for three secret killings without genuine evidence. The Program was institutionally Veyran, not a rogue nation, but a prosecutor or ally must distinguish criminal command links rather than simply assuming every citizen or uniformed officer shared the plan.
Three distinct fatal operation dossiers: fixed responsibility, unknown personal names protected
GLASS SPEAR / DOSSIER ONE: QUIET STAR — V's slain divine parent
DM historical truth: The Western Program identified a single material-world divine actor whose active protection obstructed a strategic Veyran interdiction/requisition objective. The god refused the Program's claim to control a protected work or refuge. Rhelka commissioned a compartmentalized covert action, using compromised access to a real place and a tailored ritual disruption to create one brief period of mortal vulnerability; a coalition of prepared mortal attackers inflicted the fatal injuries. Corven's instruments registered the collapse, but no power flowed directly into the attackers. The GOD's precise name, portfolio, gender if not already established, region, calendar date and personal involvement with young V are still UNESTABLISHED; settle those from independent player-approved canon and concrete field evidence before a named reveal, never invent a retroactive recollection or force a new ancestry mechanic. V's vampire father was neither the target nor automatically a participant. The operation is the cause of the god's death, hence V's Severing, not of his sealing/awakening.
GLASS SPEAR / DOSSIER TWO: COLD VERDICT — Cirren's slain divine parent
DM historical truth: A different divine actor opposed the Program's capture of specific mortals, knowledge or sacred facilities, threatening its re-education/research agenda. The Program used an intelligence-led isolation strategy: cut off trusted witnesses/defenders, lure the god to a physically vulnerable site or moment, and direct a combined martial/limited magical assault by a distinct local team. The god died from those deliberate blows; the Program falsified the incident as a local uprising or divine feud to hide Veyran instigation. Exact god name, portfolio, calendar date, sanctuary, allies and how much Cirren knew of his parent remain UNESTABLISHED and should be fixed before disclosure. This does NOT make Cirren's missing mentor, his estranged family, Quenwood, Saint Orlan's murders or Larchwater hospice a Program cell; they are independently caused.
GLASS SPEAR / DOSSIER THREE: THE LAST ROAR — Nobanion, Fenar's divine ancestor
FIXED DATE RELATION: Nobanion, the Lion King, was killed approximately six months before Day171. Fenar was hunting on a mountain when she felt his final painful roar, collapsed and was later found injured. That sensing reflects her extraordinary remote divine inheritance, not witnessing a Veyran assailant, sharing precise death coordinates or gaining a magical tracking power. The Program selected Nobanion because his protection of vulnerable communities, guardianship and ability to rally defenders obstructed its expansion/research goals. Rhelka coordinated a target-specific raid by a temporary mortal coalition with local intelligence, prepared terrain and limited arcane disruption; the killing struck while Nobanion physically intervened against a serious danger to people or his charges. The danger was engineered by Program proxies. Nobanion died resisting; the killers used the massacre to feed strategic planning and Corven's divine-death observations. The site, surviving witnesses and exact attack sequence remain sealed until legitimately found; no implication Nobanion was personally at Raukhar's Rest or that its 80–120 residents saw the raid.
Nobanion's death is an established family tragedy, not a twist that makes Fenar secretly allied with Veyr. Her actual grief, self-reference style, voluntary companion status, strength recovery and choice of vengeance/cooperation remain hers to play; V does not automatically know the same enemy killed both of their god-linked relatives. The Program is not automatically aware of Fenar's lineage or her discovery of V. The Raukhar clan has no preordained death warrant or forced militia role. The historic killers' core operation is NOT the current 24-person Hollow Pennant roster and cannot be called to the present without an actually funded/logistically viable new action.
