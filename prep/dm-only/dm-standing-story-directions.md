## Antagonist moral direction — Day172 OOC player ruling
- Ashen Regency and the active Veyran Western Approaches antagonistic leadership/operational cores are deliberately, predominantly EVIL. They may sincerely believe they seek protection, order, restoration or a better future, but their objectives include domination and their actions deliberately include coercion, terror and unjustified killing of civilians. Do not whitewash their conduct as mere misunderstandings, harmless diplomacy or necessary sacrifice. Give visible victims, tangible evidence, bad choices actively made and definitive adventure endpoints. More evil antagonists enable genuine heroism and combat without mandatory capture-only play. Keep morally complex personalities while not blurring deliberate atrocities into unproven rumors once evidence is established.
- This is an OUT-OF-CHARACTER DM direction, not a 2014 mechanical alignment rule or universal moral classification of a nationality, species or every junior member. Distinguish commanding perpetrators from innocent subjects, coercively recruited helpers, nonparticipants and particular defectors. A hostile unit attacking innocents can present real lethal danger; surrender/helpless status still matters and Cirren's refusal to execute captives is intact. Never force V's moral stance or automatically prescribe death as the right resolution. Existing completed episodes and the currently played Day172 ~7:25PM Ophira conversation are not retconned, and no game time/resources change.
