# Random encounter catalogue — Convergence ecology and encounter variety
Ruling: RANDOM-ENCOUNTER-BREADTH-D331-001, player correction at Day331 ~11:00, 2026-11-25. **Private preparation/candidate library, NOT a new world-fauna register, revealed information, ready-to-roll regional table or new encounter.** The existing gnolls and previously accepted travel remain exactly as played.

## Authority and generation
See [travel rules](../../campaign/continuity/rules-travel-communication.md) and [DM procedures/bestiary sourcing](../../campaign/continuity/rules-dm-procedures.md). Full approved 2014-rules-family catalogue remains the selection universe, not merely this seed bank. Check world foundations, politics, atlas/terrain and actual local histories first. The Convergence transplanted whole ecosystems, populations, ancient sites and civilizations ~1,200 years ago. Think of native, established introduced, interacting and displaced species; many habitat-compatible creatures can now be indigenous to local landscapes in the ordinary sense of generations of residence. Do not assign an origin, present distribution, breeding exception, god, open planar portal, new state or named faction solely because a source setting offers one.

1. During regional prep, search the indexed eligible source universe for appropriate species, profiles and encounter circumstances. Build a reusable, source-pinned regional candidate bank, including multiple habitats and creature functions; make a route-specific eligible subset for current climate, terrain, settlement, ecology, light, activity and known continuity. Aim for dozens of credible eligible creature/faction choices where geography supports them; a three-name remembered-favorites list is not adequate merely because convenient. No forced quota or unsupported ecology. If a small actual habitat really limits eligible residents, record why. Maintain an expandable long-tail pool as new areas are prepared.
2. Keep unique selection identities (related stages/variants grouped where they represent the same species), exact source version, habitat tags or explicit habitat justification, rarity/status, contextual prerequisites, activity/approach possibilities and credible severity/composition envelopes. Named sourcebook NPCs, setting-specific origin stories and divine claims are not imported as campaign canon. Check campaign exclusions and banned spells; check full mechanics and all _copy/_mod/template dependencies before live use. Missing environment tags require actual ecological judgment, not an assumption of either eligibility or exclusion. Dragons and other canon-rare or exceptional beings remain rare and individually grounded. No artificial rarity increases to force novelty.
3. Fix actual candidate weights and map ranges BEFORE rolling selection; choose the identity by a genuine weighted draw, not by post-roll DM familiarity. Simple recommended prep defaults are 6:3:1 for common/uncommon/rare **within the locally eligible set**, with optional evidence-supported adjustments for local abundance, migration, season, territory or previous sightings. For a recent repeated incidental species/encounter family, consider a modest weight reduction without overriding real population/re-encounters; do not make repeats impossible. Do not use these defaults as asserted historical population facts. A roll may select a precommitted mixed group or a related species family; don't retroactively change identity after the draw. Record reasons and exact ranges. If a catalogue pool isn't ready, prepare the scoped pool before selecting, or transparently identify the exceptional constrained source/availability problem; never claim a draw when manually selecting.
4. Independently define the encounter situation (ongoing activity, intent, location/physical layout and stakes) before the encounter is presented. Where several genuinely plausible circumstances remain open, a predeclared weighted situation draw is useful; known actor behavior, fixed adventure prep and established ecology govern instead of dice when already determined. Random activity must not soften or rewrite an intrinsically hostile encounter. Dangerous creatures may hunt, ambush, attack immediately when warranted, pursue, fight to the death, flee, guard, migrate or act according to their published traits and actual motives. No peaceful-outcome quota, compulsory sympathy, automatic remorse, or mandatory talk before combat. Variety concerns *what happens*, not removing lethal aggression.
5. Preserve the existing occurrence, type, cap and severity distributions. When actually hostile, execute severity under the existing rule. Determine credible numbers/variants, objective, starting geometry, senses, cover, pursuit/retreat and terrain before initiative. Calibrate against the actual party and level; do not replace a selected identity or inflate an impossible population to force a band. If fixed ecology or a published high-CR creature makes the intended band unachievable, keep the actual danger and disclose the mismatch rather than fudge. No automatic leveling, treasure, quest, or replacement encounter.
6. Use the same disciplined breadth for dangerous humanoids and other categories, not only monsters: encounter tables should include credible local armed institutions, independent factions, nonhuman predators and communities, predators in unusual circumstances, environmental phenomena, noncombat people and discoveries. Creature category is the encounter purpose, not solely the printed statblock type (e.g. hunting gnolls may be a dangerous humanoid group; organized trolls might be a monstrous band). Do not use one identity twice in competing rows of the same actual draw. A meaningful encounter need not be a fight or another party of armed roadblockers.
7. Cache the prepared regional tables and consult them only at relevant route/prep transitions. Roll occurrence, type, identity/situation when appropriate and severity when hostile in the actual live turn, genuine visible RNG. No pre-rolled unplayed results. Record source pins and the selected result in accepted recovery when played; preserve immutable prior outcomes. Quest Development may expand this library and generate eligible regional subsets during its existing scoped review; do not run a full bestiary crawl during combat. This seed file is not a second authority for statblocks or actual region fauna.

## Situation repertoire (not a mandatory uniform table)
Populate local weighted situation rows for each candidate's real capabilities and context. For predation/combat use prey already cornered, hunting from above/below, active lair defense, hunting at water, pack cutting off retreat, prey migration crossing a route, rival predators fighting, resource/territory conflict, night pursuit, attacking caravan or settlement, disturbance of a creature's resting site, desperate escape after real wounds. Other credible situations include seasonal migration, an enormous distant crossing, aerial/underground/underwater activity, territorial display, failed hunt, creature nesting, swarm/parasite or ecological cascade, predator feeding, feeding ground discovery. For intelligent groups also include war patrol, hostile raiders, rival hunting parties, siege/reconnaissance, occupied crossing, pursuing a third party, territorial defense, actual military/civic relief, pilgrimage, trade/foraging expeditions, migration or diplomatic contact. Select from context and known intent; not every entry is available to every creature. For hazards use flood/rockfall, wildfire, bog, lightning/ice, damaged infrastructure, genuine magical aftermath, instability of a ruin or dangerous local Convergence-linked effects where canon supports them. For social/discovery use public ceremonies, changed trade routes, local customs, war news, migrations, ruins, stranded travelers, scientific/ecological phenomena, cultural and divine legacies, existing faction activity. Don't turn them all into another rescue obligation, toll demand, conspiracy, or quest.
