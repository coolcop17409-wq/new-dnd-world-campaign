Find the ground beneath Verdana
Starting point. At Nightglass Hall, Lord Severin associated a dark, silver-veined heart with the Sanguine Sovereign and ruins of Verdana. V heard “My child.” Steward Edric Vale studied the House. The signet’s present custody, exact chronology, ancestry and title to the original seat remain open. Blackthorn Hold and its quarry Deep Heart are distinct.
Prepare. Follow the object’s journey in reverse. Edric Vale can be asked for a tracing of the carrier’s mark on its old packing. A surveyor’s field book in an Orsaven map room depicts the same mark on a hauling frame beside a collapsed crossing. The book records two banks, a surviving pier and a broken aqueduct alignment. These can place the recovery district on a later field visit. A rubbing or witnessed description suffices to open the inquiry.
At the table. At the crossing, a household of local ferriers keeps a landing on the old abutment. They know how to enter the lower vaults during low water and want the return channel left usable. Explore by their stair, rig a line outside, or wait for a better water level. An in-place Vaishnavi mason’s dedication over a service threshold answers whether the House maintained premises there. A loose object bought at market cannot establish that by itself. Prepare this as an outlying service house. When the threshold and field book are read together, they give the bearing of the former keep’s main approach.
Possible endings. Establish the first verified foothold of the ancestral estate, win limited access by protecting the ferry, or document the site from the opposite bank at lower risk and leave the vault unexplored. Reclaiming a seat takes an expedition, and living occupants have claims of their own. Save the heart’s physical identity and V’s parentage for evidence that can genuinely address them.
