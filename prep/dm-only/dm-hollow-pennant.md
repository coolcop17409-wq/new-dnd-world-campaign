Status and fixed conspiracy
DM ONLY — unintroduced preparation. An independent regional military arc, not a retroactive explanation of the theater, silk-market or Quenwood cases. The Hollow Pennant is a deniable company supplied by a Veyran Service Directorate liaison. Its field controller plans staged attacks in alternating Ossent/Crownsunder colors to provoke reprisals and divert both states from Veyr. Local mercenaries know their own raids; the controller and quartermaster know the sponsor. Existing political rivalry is real and predates them. Proving this cell does not prove every Veyran official ordered its crimes.
Organization and limits
Three physical functions: a forward raiding camp, a supply depot in a disused Orseld toll fort, and a field command/signal post. Regional company roster: twenty-four hired fighters, one quartermaster and one controller, divided among these sites and escorts; record their exact distribution before activation. Civilians and coerced porters are separate. Stores were accumulated over months through ordinary carriers. Replacements require recruitment/travel; no instant reinforcements, limitless agents or divine surveillance. The company needs provisions, false colors, pack animals and reliable signals. The mercenary captain wants payment and a survivable withdrawal; the controller wants a successful provocation and promotion. Neither knows V's private history.
Entry and first expedition
Offer one direct danger through a route actually traveled: armed raiders attacking a waystation, or a surviving courier seeking help for people held at the forward camp. State threat, travel time, commission terms if any and withdrawal options before acceptance. The arsenal's independent arms broker may later supply the same company through an ordinary buyer; this is a new prospective sale only, never a forged clue in already searched evidence. Pensioners, their paymaster and the existing stock thieves are not secretly members. The camp can be reached without taking the arsenal job.
At the waystation/camp, show a stone refuge, wagon barricade and exposed signal perch. During a raid, the company uses lethal force against defenders and threatens civilians; establish the attack through what the party can observe or credibly learn. The danger comes from its ongoing violence and planned attacks, not uniform, nationality or affiliation. Apply RULES to surrendered or helpless people and companions' independent judgments. Party can cut off the signal, breach the palisade, rescue people through a culvert or withdraw. Deliver a local victory now: survivors freed, supplies saved or a raiding unit destroyed, even if the party stops here.
Depot raid — reveal the scale
Three independent routes identify the depot: marked supply loads with a destination chit; the visible return trail of pack animals; a freed carter who personally hauled there. None requires a living enemy or successful interrogation. Routine orders reveal the depot, not the entire conspiracy at once.
The toll fort has an exposed haul ramp, a storm-cut postern and a cistern stair. Scouts can see stores, guards and exits before committing. The quartermaster maintains uniforms from both states, standardized raid loads and a dated attack model because crews require rehearsal. These are physical evidence and useful targets, not another account-book interview. Burning or carrying away raid equipment disrupts operations; freeing laborers saves lives; taking the signal kit can enable deception. Fire also threatens stored food and captives, so show their positions. A defeat or withdrawal can cost stores or warn command; it does not erase clues already secured.
Field command — stop the operation
The intended final provocation targets a staffed frontier wayhouse used by civilians from both states. Fix its location and the company's departure timetable when reconnaissance establishes readiness; no countdown begins merely because this outline was read. Before then allow scouting and qualifying rests. After an observed deployment, tell V the real time tradeoff.
The field post controls two approach routes and a sheltered signal terrace. V can raid command, destroy the signal chain, intercept the departing force or warn defenders with credible evidence. The controller keeps sponsor instructions in Primordial and the operational briefing in Common; comprehension alone does not authenticate an official origin. A matched supply remittance and dispatch seal can independently corroborate the connection. Killing the controller does not destroy those physical records. Success stops this operation and breaks its local command/logistics. Exposure, escape of a leader and recovery of property are separate outcomes; stopping the attack never requires a public trial.
Consequences, rewards and closure
Failure can mean a specific wayhouse attack, displaced households or an escaped force, not automatic continent-wide war. News and political reactions take real travel and depend on recipients. A thwarted company cannot regenerate offscreen without new support. Recoverable arms/supplies, an agreed military or civilian commission, safe passage and a local ally are possible rewards; establish ownership and terms before granting them. Defeating the cell is a complete regional victory. The established Directorate can remain a larger power without a compulsory trail of replacement masterminds. Further action against Veyr is a new player choice.

Fixed culpability of the Hollow Pennant (DM ONLY; unplayed)
The controller, captain and a culpable operational majority of the 24-fighter company KNOW their raids deliberately endanger and kill civilians, disguise their identity as the rival side, and drive communities into fear for political reward. They are not merely decent soldiers who accidentally wore an unfamiliar uniform. One bounded PREVIOUS unplayed-site atrocity is a staged raid on a drovers' shelter with deliberate killing of three unarmed carters by an existing detachment to remove witnesses. Evidence may be obtained independently from survivor testimony, shelter remains, supply/issue records and the physical two-color uniforms. Do not silently add troops or retroactively insert clues into earlier investigations.
These actions ground plausible decisive self-defense and assaults on active raiders. Individual employees, conscripts, civilian porters, and surrendered foes are treated according to actual conduct and 2014 rules, not blanket affiliation; Cirren does not approve killing helpless prisoners. Defeating the company stops a real series of atrocities without another mandatory mastermind. Any later Rhelka challenge is voluntary and requires credible evidence and travel.
