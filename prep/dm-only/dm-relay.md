Carry the blue cup
Status and scope
EXPIRED EDITION: the Day171 opportunity is no longer current on Day199. No winner, V participation, fee or penalty is established. The course below is a reusable unplayed design, not a race held waiting for V. Offer a later edition only with an actual organizer, future date, entry window and rules.
Carry the blue cup • A relay over three bridges
A child carries the battered blue prize cup ahead of the runners so the winner can drink from it at the finish. The western carters have one runner short. A rival team of servants from the palace kitchens has been getting up before dawn to train. The prize is the winners’ supper and the right to choose next time’s course. Children know every champion by their worst fall. Everyone has somewhere to be afterward. V can race, choose a route for another runner, back a team, or watch.
Show the course before entries close. Use three selected crossings once mapped. Adapt a leg to a quay stair or ferry if established geography calls for it. The first crossing has open paving and a long approach. The second has steps down to a quay path that saves distance when the unloading chain is still. At the third, runners choose the steep stair or a longer ramp suitable for carts. Each team carries a knotted cord and hands it to a partner at a marked post. Ask which leg and risk V wants. Offer a route estimate, let training and reconnaissance matter, and settle V’s leg against its actual runner. Mark the lead or gap at the handoff. Resolve the other legs from their runners, routes and established conditions so V’s sprint can win a leg on a losing team. Establish the house rules of the race, including any magic restrictions, before entry.
A delayed flour wagon or the funeral from the noble quarter can change one approach if it is actually there. Warning both teams earns goodwill and surrenders a tactical advantage. Fast play, careful scouting and an ordinary clean loss all produce a finish and a supper. If V misses the start, the results stand. Let this edition finish, including a clean defeat. The teams choose their next course over supper. A carrier V meets here may later offer a paid route or forward a message to Sabine by arrangement.
