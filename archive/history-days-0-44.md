## Days 0–44: Eastwatch, the road and Blackthorn proposal
**0–12, Eastwatch:** V awakened from an ancient Orath tomb as a Reborn of an ancient, unknown species (older 'high elf' description retconned) with fragmented memories, inherited silver sword and Seventh Seat connection. With Mara, Rusk, Torren and Tessa, exposed quarry/Archive-Seven operations; captured Hadrik Vennor, three guards surrendered, four laborers rescued. Reclaimed gallery/throne authority, prepared and performed severance, released spirit to rest. Individual hearings released golden A-01 and unrepentant A-03; bodiless A-02 placed with awakened researcher Elias Thorne; manipulative A-04 isolated in V's capsule. Merrow expedition: Garron killed by machinery, Holt injured/recovered, Senn rescued/detained, Pell fled. Merrow captured; coercive claims remain qualified. Evidence to Magistracy,300 gp reward; sales and companion equipment reconciled to989 gp2 sp after Long Rest. Mistaken Odran25 gp charge reversed. One separate ancient-coin gift with Mara; no healing potion canon.

Extended travel retiming in RULES creates no extra purchases/resources.
