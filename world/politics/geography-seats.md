# Known hemisphere and geographic scale
The approximately Earth-sized world's developed setting lies in the known hemisphere: Arden northwest, Orath northeast and east, and Namarra south of the Meeting Seas. Named states occupy only portions of these continents; additional societies, wilderness, and outer regions remain undeveloped. Adopted scale bands and placements constrain mapping, while precise coastlines and borders remain open.
| Landmass | Developed regions |
| - | - |
| Arden | Iron Dominion, Kheldorn, Talveren, Aundar, Zafira, Oathmark, Talassar and Charter Coast holdings, Ashcrown, Frostbound |
| Namarra | Open Table Accord, Lastlight, Elderweald, Vathrune, Thornveil, Talassar and Charter Coast holdings; Vhalzareth and neighbors underground |
| Broken Isles | Blackweir and other independent island communities |
| Orath | Veyr, Ossent, Crownsunder, Drusk, Allhaven, Ilvaran, Dunwall; Stormhold offshore |
The Meeting Seas collectively name the connected waters between the three continents. The Greyreach Sea lies between Arden and Orath; the Serean Sea between Arden and Namarra; the Avel Sea between Orath and Namarra. The Vigil Sea is the regional sea around Lastlight and the facing northwestern Namarra coast, opening into the Serean Sea; the Lantern Strait is a particular passage within it. The Summer Sea remains associated with warmer southern routes around Namarra. The Broken Isles are a major central island chain of mixed origin, with independent communities and contested harbors, not a unified state.
The Unbounded Ocean separates the known hemisphere from the far hemisphere. Mainland societies collectively call its outer archipelagos the Farwake Isles; island peoples have their own names, charts, and networks. Coastal waters are regularly navigated, outer island routes require regional knowledge, and deep crossings are rare and imperfectly documented. The far hemisphere contains its own societies and maps; it is not universally undiscovered.

## Terrain and Convergence seams
Natural processes underpin geography: collision mountains, erosion, deposition, glaciation, rift basins, volcanic island arcs, and rain shadows. The Convergence interrupted these patterns. Central seas include ancient rift basins enlarged by Convergence flooding; outer archipelagos combine volcanic islands with transplanted rock, ecosystems, and ruins. The regional sections below describe the resulting terrain after 1,200 years of adaptation.
Most developed regions have temperate climates, while continental extensions can reach tropical and polar latitudes. Earth-like circulation, wetter windward slopes, and drier rain shadows inform the climate. Exact latitudes and prevailing-wind placement remain open. Outward and homeward ship journeys can take different lengths of time.
Local Convergence anomalies follow the Scarland rules below; no continent-wide zone of arbitrary magical instability is established.

## Climate seasons and territorial control
Cold uplands and high mountains have snowy winters, closed passes, thaw floods, and unstable slopes. Dominion plains have warm growing seasons and winter storage needs. Aundar and Talveren vary sharply with elevation; sheltered valleys and higher settlements harvest at different times. Zafira has seasonal rains and irrigation demands; Talassar and the Charter Coast retain mild warm-temperate conditions with dry summers and wetter, stormier winters.
Accord and Lastlight have maritime rain, fog, and storms, with coastal temperatures moderated by maritime air and a warm current. Elderweald remains moist with particular magical departures. Namarra's southern desert is largely a mountain rain shadow; Thornveil has a watered coastal foothill pocket and catchments supplemented by imports. Orath has temperate lowlands, colder Drusk uplands, and stronger interior extremes. Most inhabitants know dependable local seasons. A few Convergence pockets have enduring magical exceptions; exact latitude and wind placement remain open within the adopted climates.
Settled heartlands have regular courts, taxes, officials, and roads. Outlying holdings depend on local protection. Frontier claims overlap and follow patrols and settlement. Remote wilderness can contain independent communities and other powers even where claimed by a state. Underground control follows inhabited chambers, entrances, passages, and water systems; surface borders do not automatically extend downward.
Maps should use firm boundaries for well-held or negotiated territory, uncertain boundaries for contested frontiers, and substantial areas outside national colors. Underdark entrances occur across all three continents without implying a continuous practicable underground network. Convergence seams include abruptly ending mountains, transplanted gorges, and drowned roads and settlements; after 1,200 years most landscapes support functioning ecosystems and drainage.
## Maritime routes and open placements
The Meeting Seas contain deep basins, bays, mixed-origin islands, and drowned coastlines. Continental straits attract shipping and naval competition but have longer alternatives. Outer coasts face more exposed ocean. Island stops offer supplies, repairs, and shelter; direct crossings can be faster in favorable weather. Local coastal sailing and intercontinental passage coexist.
Principal seats and the named major ports are established in the settlement register. Exact coastlines, local borders, river bends and mouths, precise city sites, and additional ports remain open. Adopted orientation and scale guide later mapping.
