# Political Reference — topic index
This reference records the established societies, geography, history, religion, languages, and institutions of New DnD World. Named powers are not an exhaustive inventory. The world foundation is sufficient for play; further local detail can be developed as needed.
The campaign uses D&D 5e 2014 and adapted Forgotten Realms lore. Explicit user decisions govern canon. This reference owns written world lore and the evidence for adopted geography, history, politics, religion, cultures, languages, and institutions. The campaign atlas owns the player-facing map and place register. The Continuity Pack's `RULES` tab owns campaign mechanics, house rules, and how they interact with 2014 D&D. Current character stats and resources belong in the live character Sheets. Cultural descriptions concern institutions and contested ideals, not universal personalities. Operational estate accounts, ownership/claim status, agreements and schedules belong in the existing Property workbook: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VYY8lidhkDe6iiCaY8uEesg1ocxunXIE_Q6Y9-baw9Q/edit#gid=30001 (Estates directory). Use each named estate's registers; Blackthorn's tax and Carrow terms are not general world law or automatic terms for future holdings.
