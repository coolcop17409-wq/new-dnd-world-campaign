# Open decisions and continuity safeguards
**Campaign choices and unknowns:** V's extinct original species and exceptional compatibility are adopted, though its name/history and any living mixed descendants remain unknown. Fenar's Nobanion ancestry and recent Godslaying/Severing are adopted; cause and perpetrator unknown. V/Cirren divine parents and specific other recent Godslayings remain open. Do not retcon undeveloped worldbuilding options into canon. Tempus, Umberlee and Beshaba are NOT established recent deaths.
**Local development as needed:** additional rulers, population figures, internal factions, network branches, city layouts, and unnamed settlements. Existing capitals, Veyran leaders, drow matrons, and the Dominion emperor's background remain established.
- Continue refining coastlines, river bends and mouths, precise sites for named seats and ports, latitude, and prevailing winds within the adopted continental orientations, scale bands, regional placements, and named geography. The 150-entry minor-polity atlas layer is now documented as a working proposal set; remaining review concerns its boundary fit and geographic placement, not filling every blank space with another state. Cluster settled groups around habitable coasts, river corridors, and major-state frontiers; keep Arctic reaches, deserts, Underdark regions, and dragonborn buffers sparse. Minor entities must not overlap one another; contested lines may overlap major-state claims. Align major-state borders with continent outlines and preserve proportionate mountains, coherent drainage, dispersed Underdark entrances, and substantial wilderness.
- D199 adopted regional constraint (D199-SECONDARY-CHANNEL-CANON-001; further refined by D199-MIREGATE-UPPER-ROUTE-001): Orsaven's significant main river trade access is active and protected; the same older, still locally used SECONDARY river carrying water through Miregate → Sedgeford above Reedbed → Reedbed → Old Distributary Fort → Harrowmere has a farther UPSTREAM navigable valley approaching Crownsunder's hills. A useful upper landing/transfer point is approximately FOUR WAGON DAYS (route time, not fixed miles) from Blackthorn Hold by a traversable hilly wagon road; rocky slopes/ridges and steep upstream water block direct shipping to Blackthorn, which stays principally LAND TRADE. The Compact's armed occupation was comparatively recent and centered on the weaker recovered works, NOT the entire main river/upper valley. CONFIRMED geographic relationship is SAME Miregate river, NOT a different tentative feeder; earlier hypothetical 1–2 wagon days is superseded. Still OPEN: river names, exact landing/route coordinates and official status, source/mouth/confluence, upstream/downstream boat times, whether/how Orsaven's main channel joins, maritime outlet, decline causes/dates, precise Compact occupation dates and Crown responses. No Crown blindness, automatic Blackthorn dock, freight contract, new revenue, toll right, or connection to Dornal/Veyr.
**Divine history:** Bane's opponent and Edrin's connection; Lathander's battlefield enemy and circumstances; reasons for Corellon's hiding and Yondalla and Garl's disappearance; Maglubiyet's death date; and the cause of forest gnome divergence remain open.
- Refine the dispersed dragonborn states' borders, leaders, Great Wyrm ages, capture dates, and custodial disputes. Define Vathrune's resident representation in the compact and the distribution of Tiamat's fragments. The Vanquisher's six-year term and existing election rules remain established.
- Develop the exact geography and leaders of Vhalzareth's five neighboring communities, the practical operation of distant elven treaties, and unresolved Cisterns and trade-route disputes.
- For Veyr, leave active searches, specific revelations, divine targets, and expedition plots open for campaign development. Resolve Bane's hand's exact reach, ritual limits, preservation requirements, and possible surviving awareness. Specific later divine acquisitions remain open. For the Dominion, resolve the emperor's proposed name Vorgath and epithet the Provisioner, specific divine empowerments and sites, the chronology of Maglubiyet's death, other divine victims, and the unnamed conquered province's geography. Its placement must not turn Kheldorn's localized conflict into an established full invasion.
**Open Warden decisions:** responses to occupation and fragment trafficking, ranks, chapterhouses, team structures, internal politics, additional equipment, detailed treaty powers, and exact Hand mechanics remain open. These are optional development subjects, not prerequisites to play.
Superseded proposals are not canon: a 2,000-year Convergence; adjacent elven states; automatic democracy after Bane; Lastlight predating Lathander's death; one territorial dragonborn federation; young captive dragons; a mobile or technically precise Bane detector; special Veyran fragment-binding expertise; effective defensive coalitions; total war at Kheldorn; or a mature Dunwall resistance.
Ancient state identities do not imply unchanged borders or dynasties. Predominant ancestry does not imply homogeneity. Unadopted examples remain possibilities. Further worldbuilding is optional and should serve play.
